Hisab: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Hindi version below

Hisab ("the app") is a record-keeping app for small shops, made by Abhishek Sharma ("we"). This page explains what the app stores, where it goes, and the choices you have. Questions: tost9870@gmail.com.

1. What the app stores

  • On your phone: your shop profile, customers, suppliers, khata (ledger) entries, bills, purchases, items and stock, expenses, rent, milk-delivery and repair records, and settings. The app works fully offline and keeps this in a private database on your phone.
  • Your account: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address to identify your account. We never see your Google password.
  • In the cloud (only after you sign in): the business records above, so you can use your shop on more than one phone and keep a copy if a phone is lost. They are stored on servers of our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare. Customer names and mobile numbers that you enter are part of these records.
  • Photos: photos you attach to sold items or expenses are kept on your phone and, when you are signed in, in private cloud storage. Only people in your shop can open them, through short-lived private links.
  • Alerts: to send notifications (low stock, dues, a bill cancelled by someone else) we keep a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) for each phone you use.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not collect your location, contacts or SMS.
  • WhatsApp and SMS messages are prepared on your phone and are sent only when you press send in your own WhatsApp or SMS app.

3. Camera, notifications and screen lock

  • Camera: only to scan barcodes and to take photos of items or receipts.
  • Notifications: for the alerts above. You can switch each kind off in Settings.
  • Screen lock: the optional App Lock asks for your phone's own PIN, pattern or fingerprint. We never receive or store it.

4. Backups and sharing

  • You may back up to your own Google Drive (the app can only see the backup files it creates) or export an Excel or backup file. The app asks for your consent before a backup leaves your phone.
  • Other people see your shop only if you add them. You choose their role: owner sees everything; manager does not see buying prices, profit, suppliers or purchases; cashier and staff also do not see expenses, rent or exports. These limits are enforced on our server, not only on screen.

5. How we use data

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in, keep your shop in sync between your phones, store your photos, and send the alerts you asked for. We use service providers to do this (Cloudflare for servers and storage; Google for sign-in and notifications). They process data for us and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes.

6. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • One shop's data is separated from every other shop's, and the server checks who you are and your role on every request.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes on our server, and a switched-off or removed person loses access at once.

7. How long we keep data, and deleting it

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • Delete your account and cloud data: in the app, go to More → Cloud backup and sync → Delete cloud account and confirm with Google. Your cloud records and photos are then deleted permanently.
  • Delete the data on your phone: Settings → Delete my data, or uninstall the app.
  • Full steps for deleting your account and data: delete-account page.
  • If you cannot use the app, email tost9870@gmail.com from your sign-in address and we will delete your account and data.

8. Children

Hisab is a business tool and is not meant for children under 18.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

10. Contact
